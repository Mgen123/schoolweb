Academic Programs & Tracks

Discover the educational pathways that shape your future at Ipilan National High School

Junior High School Program

Building strong foundations for academic excellence and personal development

Grade 7 - Foundation Year

Building fundamental skills and adapting to high school environment with exploratory subjects.

  • Core subjects: English, Math, Science
  • Filipino and Social Studies
  • Technology and Livelihood Education
  • Music, Arts, Physical Education
  • Values Education

Grade 8 - Exploratory Year

Expanding knowledge base with more specialized subjects and skill development.

  • Advanced core subjects
  • Introduction to specialized fields
  • Enhanced TLE offerings
  • Computer Education
  • Career guidance orientation

Grade 9 - Specialization Start

Beginning of track exploration with more focused elective subjects.

  • Specialized TLE tracks
  • Advanced Science and Math
  • Research introduction
  • Arts and Sports development
  • Leadership training

Grade 10 - Preparation Year

Final preparation for Senior High School with career path decision making.

  • Career assessment
  • Senior High School orientation
  • Research project completion
  • Work immersion preparation
  • College and career planning
