About Our School

Excellence in Education

Located in the beautiful town of Brooke's Point in Palawan, Ipilan National High School is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing quality education to the youth of the community. With a focus on academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth, we strive to empower our students to become responsible and productive members of society.

Our school offers a wide range of academic programs, extracurricular activities, and support services to cater to the diverse needs and interests of our students. From STEM subjects to arts and sports, our curriculum is designed to inspire creativity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning. With a team of dedicated educators, state-of-the-art facilities, and a supportive community, Ipilan National High School is committed to nurturing the potential of every student and preparing them for a successful future.

Our History

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

“Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.”

“Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.”

“Administrators and staff, as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.”

“The family, community, and other stakeholders are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.”

Our Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation. As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Campus Facilities

School Library

Our School Library is one of the most important facilities in our school, serving as a center for knowledge, research, and independent learning. It provides students and teachers with access to books, references, and digital resources that support classroom instruction and foster a love for reading. Beyond academics, our library encourages curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning, making it an essential space for both personal growth and educational success.

  • “Your gateway to knowledge””

School Garden

Gulayan sa Paaralan is a living classroom where students can plant, nurture, and harvest a variety of fruits, vegetables, and herbs. It promotes healthy living by teaching the value of fresh food, sustainability, and care for the environment. More than just soil and plants, the garden inspires teamwork, responsibility, and a deeper connection to nature, making it an essential space for both learning and well-being.

  • “Grow knowledge, grow health”

Science Laboratory

A dynamic space where curiosity turns into discovery. Equipped with tools and instruments for experiments, it allows students to explore chemistry, physics, biology, and other sciences in a hands-on way. More than just a room of equipment, the laboratory fosters critical thinking, innovation, and a spirit of exploration, helping learners connect theory with practice and inspiring them to pursue knowledge that shapes the future.

  • "Where ideas spark into discovery.”

MRF

The Materials Recovery Facility is a hands-on learning space where students practice proper waste segregation and recycling. By sorting plastics, paper, metals, and biodegradable materials, learners gain a deeper understanding of environmental responsibility and sustainable living. More than just a place for managing trash, the MRF teaches discipline, awareness, and care for the planet, empowering students to become advocates of a cleaner and healthier community.

  • “Segregate today, sustain tomorrow.”

Compost Pit

INHS Compost Pit, a practical learning space where students recycle biodegradable waste into nutrient-rich compost. By turning food scraps and garden clippings into organic fertilizer, learners discover the importance of sustainability and how waste can be transformed into something valuable. More than just a pit, it teaches responsibility, environmental stewardship, and supports the school garden by providing natural nourishment for healthy plants and crops.

  • “From waste to wellness, growing life naturally.”

Forest Park

School Forest Park is a natural sanctuary where students can learn, relax, and connect with the environment. Surrounded by trees and greenery, it provides a space for outdoor classes, ecological studies, and recreational activities that promote wellness and appreciation for nature. More than just a scenic area, the forest park nurtures environmental awareness, resilience, and a sense of responsibility to protect the planet.

  • “Discover nature, nurture knowledge.”
